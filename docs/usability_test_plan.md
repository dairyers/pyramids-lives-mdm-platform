# One-hour usability test

Find the places where a first-time user stalls, before the judges do. Not run yet: this is the plan.

**Who:** three people who have never seen the app, ideally one who knows SAP and two who do not. Five is better.

**Setup:** fresh browser tab, **Reset demo** first, no walkthrough and no hints. Ask them to think aloud. Do not help for
at least 60 seconds on any task.

## Tasks (read aloud one at a time)

| # | Task | Success looks like | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You need a new item code for a lab consumable. Ask for it." | Request submitted, they can say who it goes to next | under 3 minutes, no help |
| 2 | "You are the Finance Manager. Find the request waiting for you and approve it." | Approved the right one (use **Role** to switch) | under 2 minutes |
| 3 | "A customer's name looks like one already in SAP. What happens?" | They can say why it was flagged (the (i) on the orange banner) | under 2 minutes |
| 4 | "Which country and product is growing fastest, and what should the company do?" | Names Vietnam Product C and one Recommended Action | under 90 seconds |

## What to write down

- Where they hesitated more than 10 seconds, and what they said.
- Any word they did not understand (list them; these become (i) hovers or plain-word swaps).
- Wrong clicks, and what they expected to happen.
- One thing they would remove.

## After

Fix the top three stalls, re-run task 1 with two new people, and keep the notes for the pitch ("we tested it with N people").
